United Nations Sustainable Development Goals

Why HerSafe AI aligns with SDG 5

SDG 5 aims to achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls. Its Target 5.2 specifically calls for eliminating violence against women and girls in public and private spheres, including sexual and other forms of exploitation.

5GENDER EQUALITY

Technology as an access layer

HerSafe AI uses conversational AI to lower the friction involved in documenting an experience and finding appropriate support. It is designed to complement, not replace, formal reporting systems, qualified professionals, or emergency services.

SDG 5.2
Eliminate all forms of violence against all women and girls in the public and private spheres.
SDG 5.b
Enhance the use of enabling technology to promote the empowerment of women.

What HerSafe AI contributes

Our MVP focuses on four practical contributions:

Accessible documentation: conversational reporting instead of a complicated form.
AI-assisted understanding: extracts and organizes information from an unstructured narrative.
Support navigation: suggests relevant categories of help and next steps.
User agency: the user reviews and decides what to do with a generated report.
